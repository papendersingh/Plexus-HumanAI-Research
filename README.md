Plexus HumanAI Labs

Understanding Humans. Advancing AI.

Plexus HumanAI Labs is an early-stage independent research initiative focused on human-AI interaction and human behavior in the context of emerging AI systems.

Research Focus

We aim to study how people:

* Communicate with AI systems
* Interpret and evaluate AI-generated responses
* Develop trust or skepticism toward AI
* Question, disagree with, or follow AI recommendations
* Respond emotionally to AI interactions
* Make decisions when AI systems provide information or advice

Research Objectives

The initiative seeks to develop evidence-based insights that can contribute to more human-centered, context-aware, and responsible AI systems.

Particular areas of interest include:

* Human-AI trust and reliance
* Over-reliance and inappropriate confidence
* Uncertainty and disagreement
* Communication styles and user responses
* Behavioral differences across interaction contexts
* Human factors relevant to AI safety and alignment

Research Approach

Our planned research framework emphasizes:

* Voluntary participation
* Informed consent
* Privacy-conscious research practices
* Anonymized and structured responses
* Controlled AI interaction scenarios
* Qualitative and quantitative analysis

The initiative is currently at the research-design and early development stage. We do not claim established findings at this stage.

Current Project

Human Behavior Intelligence for Next-Generation AI

The project explores how variations in AI responses may influence human interpretation, trust, decision-making, and willingness to follow AI-generated recommendations.

Ethics

Plexus HumanAI Labs follows the principle:

Study human behavior with people — not secretly about people.

Research activities are intended to respect participant autonomy, informed consent, privacy, and responsible data practices.
